The Concept

One journey. Five coasts.
Countless flavours.

Guests "board" at Mumbai's C.S.T. and travel India's western coastline, stopping at five distinct food cultures before arriving in Kerala's Malabar. Every stop is a flavour zone on the menu, a design cue in the restaurant, and a chapter in one continuous brand story — built to travel as naturally to Dubai or Singapore as it does across India.
